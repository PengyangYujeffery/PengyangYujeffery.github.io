About Me

Hello! I am Pengyang Yu, a PhD student in the School of Computer Science at University College Dublin.

My research focuses on auditing and monitoring the reliability and fairness of medical foundation models across clinical settings.

For academic inquiries, please contact me at pengyang.yu@ucdconnect.ie.

Explore my research · View publications

Research Highlights

01

Reliability across clinical settings

Auditing medical foundation and vision-language models under domain shift, with attention to localized reliability, calibration, and high-confidence errors.

02

Fairness in medical AI

Studying dual fairness in federated medical imaging: fairness across collaborating institutions and fairness across patient groups.

03

Context-aware deployment

Developing monitoring, calibration, and fair deferral strategies that help clarify when, where, and for whom medical AI can be trusted.
